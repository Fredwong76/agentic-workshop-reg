# Workshop Session 5: Adoption Strategy & NWOW Rollout

## **Overview**
Session 5 shifts focus from technical implementation to organizational impact. Participants will learn how to translate their individual "Agentic Wins" into a team-wide strategy. We will define "New Ways of Working" (NWOW) and establish a roadmap for the 24-Day Immersive Project required for the EDB Training Grant.

---

## **1. Learning Objectives**
*   **Workflow Identification:** Learn to distinguish between "human-only," "human-led," and "agent-led" tasks.
*   **Change Management:** Understand the psychological barriers to AI adoption and how to mitigate them.
*   **Team Topology:** Define how teams should reorganize to include AI agents as "Digital Coworkers."
*   **Strategic Roadmap:** Develop a 30/60/90 day execution plan for team scaling.

---

## **2. Session Schedule (3.0 Hours)**

### **Module 1: The NWOW Paradigm (45 Minutes)**
*   **Presenting the Shift:** From "Doing the Work" to "Directing the Work."
*   **The Architect Model:** How senior engineers leverage agents to handle boilerplate and lower-level logic.
*   **Case Study:** A 3-hour prototype vs. a 3-week manual build.

### **Module 2: The Team Adoption Workshop (90 Minutes)**
*   **Activity:** Completing the **Team Adoption Canvas**.
*   **Breakout Groups:** Mapping team workflows and identifying the "Top 3" automation targets.
*   **Gap Analysis:** Identifying data, API, and skill gaps that hinder team-wide rollout.

### **Module 3: Scaling & Governance Roadmap (45 Minutes)**
*   **EDB Grant Integration:** Explaining the 24-day immersion requirements.
*   **Audit Readiness:** How to log and document AI-assisted progress for the $300k grant claim.
*   **Next Steps:** Setting the date for the Capstone Project kickoff.

---

## **3. Key Deliverables for This Session**
1.  [ ] **Completed Team Adoption Canvas:** One per team/participant.
2.  [ ] **Pilot Project Definition:** A clearly scoped "First Win" for the team to execute in 30 days.
3.  [ ] **30/60/90 Day Roadmap:** A simplified timeline for scaling capabilities.

---

## **4. Required Materials**
*   **Team Adoption Canvas Template** (Markdown/Printout).
*   **Strategic Value Presentation Slide Deck.**
*   **EDB Training Grant Overview PDF.**

---

## **5. Discussion Prompts**
*   *"What is the single biggest fear your team has about using Agentic IDEs?"*
*   *"If you could automate 20% of your team's weekly meetings or reporting, what would you do with that reclaimed time?"*
*   *"How will we verify 'Quality' when code is generated 10x faster?"*
